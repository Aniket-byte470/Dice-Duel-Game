🎲 # Dice-Duel-Game

🕹️ How to Play
Roll the Dice: Click the "Roll Dice" button to roll a die.​
Accumulate Points: If you roll a number between 2 and 6, it's added to your current turn's score.​
Avoid Rolling a 1: Rolling a 1 resets your current turn's score, and your turn ends.​
Hold Your Score: Click "Hold" to add your current turn's score to your total score, then pass the turn to your opponent.​
Win the Game: Be the first player to reach 100 points to win!

🚀 Features
Interactive gameplay with real-time score updates.​
Responsive design suitable for various devices.​
Visual feedback indicating active player and winner.​
Option to reset the game and start anew.​
